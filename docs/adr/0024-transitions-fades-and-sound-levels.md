# 0024 — Transitions, fades and sound levels

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-07

## Context
Prompt 14 adds fine sound editing (clip gain, volume curves, fades with shapes, track volume and pan), cross-fades between adjacent clips for sound and picture, and the display of silences and gaps so cuts (made by hand or by an AI agent) can be adjusted. Open questions: where transitions live in the document and how they survive the item edits around them while every operation keeps an exact inverse; what a fade shape means for sound and for pictures; how volume automation becomes Web Audio scheduling; how a dissolve composites without darkening; and what the timeline needs from the core to show silences.

## Decision
- **Transitions belong to their track.** `Track.transitions: Vec<Transition { id, kind: crossDissolve | audioCrossfade, leftItem, rightItem, duration, alignment: center | start | end }>`; items do not refer to them. A transition sits on the cut where `leftItem` ends and `rightItem` starts; its span is split around the cut (`Transition::extent`: half each way when centred, the odd flick after the cut; all after for `start`, all before for `end`). During the span the outgoing item plays past its end and the incoming one before its start, using their **handles** (`item_handles`: the source left beyond the clip's source range divided by its speed; unlimited for stills, texts, shapes and media of unknown duration after the end).
- **Rules are document invariants** (`validate_project`, geometry in `document::transition_fit`): both items on the track and adjacent, kind matching the track kind, positive duration, span inside both items, at most one transition per item edge and no overlap inside a shared item (`INVALID_TRANSITION`), enough media beyond the edges (`INSUFFICIENT_HANDLES`). The operations `AddTransition`, `UpdateTransition`, `RemoveTransition` only check ids, indices and locks; whether a transition fits is validated once at the end of the transaction, so a transaction may add one before the edits that make it fit.
- **Transitions follow their items.** The primitive item moves (`placement::place_item`, `remove_item`) remove the transitions of the touched item that no longer fit and record an `AddTransition` at the same index after the `RestoreItem`/`AddItem` of the inverse, so every step stays exactly invertible and inverses replay one by one. Edits made of steps — `SplitItem`, `RippleDeleteRange`, `InsertGap`, and se-app's `EditPlan::finish` for whole gestures — then put back the transitions they removed whose items touch again (`TransitionSnapshot::restorations`, shortened to what the cut allows, the left item renamed to the right part of a split); their inverses go first in the inverse list. Moving both items together, ripple-trimming at the cut or cutting the outgoing clip therefore keeps the transition; moving one item away or opening a gap removes it.
- **Durations are fitted by the use cases.** `addTransitions` and `updateTransition` shorten the requested duration to `transition_room` (handles, item lengths, neighbouring transitions); cuts without room are skipped, `TRANSITION_NO_ROOM` when none has room. A gesture or an agent asking for too long a cross-fade gets the longest that fits.
- **Fade shapes are names; the engine owns the curves.** `MediaClip.fadeInShape/fadeOutShape: linear | constantPower | logarithmic` (default linear, so older documents read: schema version 4 with a migration adding them explicitly). `fadeCurve` (engine): `p`, `sin(pπ/2)`, and even in decibels over 60 dB (`10^(−3(1−p))`), exactly 0 and 1 at the ends. The same curve drives the sound gain and the picture opacity; levels beyond the item's edges (played through a transition) hold the edge value.
- **Volume stays in decibels.** `volumeDb: Animatable<f64>` is interpolated in decibels like any animatable property, within −96..+24 dB (`VALUE_OUT_OF_RANGE`; −96 dB is heard as silence).
- **Gain envelopes, then Web Audio ramps.** `clipGainAt(clip, time)` = `dbToGain(volume)` × fades × cross-fade weight (constant power `cos`/`sin` for sound cross-fades). For each scheduled voice (≤ one second of media), `gainEnvelope` returns one point when the gain holds, else points every 10 ms that the voice's `GainNode` follows with `linearRampToValueAtTime` (within a fraction of a decibel of the curve). The conversion is pure and tested; the graph only plays it. Audio clips joined by a sound cross-fade are extended to `playStart`/`playEnd`.
- **Dissolves composite as isolated additive groups.** During a cross dissolve, `evaluate` gives both items of the track, weighted `1 − p` and `p` and tagged with their `DissolveRole`. The Pixi compositor draws the pair into an isolated group (an `AlphaFilter` at alpha 1 renders it to its own texture), the incoming side with the premultiplied `add` blend, then composites the group normally: the result is exactly `(1 − p)·(A over below) + p·(B over below)`, with no dip to the lower tracks halfway (a golden test checks both the blend and the lower track).
- **Silences are read on demand.** `getAudioSilences { assetId }` returns the silent stretches of the stored audio analysis (source time; `null` while not analysed or after a relink to other content). The timeline fetches them once per analysis result and hatches them on audio clips (a toolbar toggle).
- **Timeline gestures** follow ADR-0021: fade handles, volume line and keyframes, transition edges and cut handles write the ephemeral gesture preview (`clipEdits`, `transition`) and send one command on release. Clicks: Ctrl/⌘ on the volume line adds a keyframe, Alt on a keyframe removes it, a click on a cut adds a transition of the default length (machine preference), `Ctrl/⌘+T` adds one at the cut nearest the playhead on each unlocked track (or the tracks of the selection).

## Consequences
- AI agents and the UI share the same rules: a transition can never be left dangling or overlapping, and edits around it never fail because of it.
- The journal format changed only by addition (`transitions`, fade shapes default when absent): older journals replay unchanged.
- A sound cross-fade and a picture dissolve are separate transitions on their own tracks; `Ctrl/⌘+T` adds both for linked clips in one undo step.
- Shrinking happens only when edits put a transition back (composites and edit plans); a simple trim that keeps a cut adjacent but steals handles removes it.
- Audio track headers show volume and pan only when the track is tall enough (56 px); the inspector offers the clip gain and fades.

## Alternatives considered
- **Transitions referenced by items (`item.transitionIn/Out`)**: two places to keep in step and no natural owner for the pair; the track owns its cuts.
- **Validating handles only in the operations**: relinking or item edits could still leave invalid documents; a document rule plus upkeep keeps every stored state valid.
- **Removing broken transitions once at the end of `apply_all`**: the inverses could not be replayed one by one (intermediate states of composite inverses would remove transitions that should stay).
- **Dangling (inactive) transitions kept when their items separate**: hidden state that agents and users would not understand.
- **Dissolve as outgoing at full opacity with the incoming over it at `p`**: exact only for opaque pictures; translucent titles would pop at the end.
- **One `setValueCurveAtTime` per voice**: equivalent sampling but cannot coexist with other automation events and offers no constant shortcut; points with linear ramps keep a constant gain as one value.
